Why Adwuma Nkabom

We saw a gap we could close.

Talented people across Ghana were ready to work, but the opportunities were scattered across WhatsApp groups, noticeboards and word of mouth. We built a single, trusted place where job seekers, employers and volunteer organisations find each other — quickly and directly.
